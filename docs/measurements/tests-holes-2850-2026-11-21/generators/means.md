- **The tests at reader time take nothing the compiler's own tests'
  programs warn of**, by the rule that a test's verdict is one piece of
  code at both times and the faster forms only list for it.
- **What the machine reads of an object is what the reader has before the
  bodies**, or it refuses: a unit with a rename pragma has its library
  builtins' calls refused, and one that declares a function multiversioned
  by target strings its calls.
- **The inputs that say "exact" are five now**: the corpora, the fixtures,
  the one-statement programs, the generator's, and the compiler's own
  tests' programs with a trigger a warning. The last is the cheapest and
  found the most. In the shadow it is a campaign's sweep, in the record;
  with the first compile behind its option the tables' own drivers run
  through the machine, and it stands by itself.
