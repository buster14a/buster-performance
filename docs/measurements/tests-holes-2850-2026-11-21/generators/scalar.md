- **A record sent to the census's code** is asked there, one at a time, as
  before: the new test is a rule in that code. What the loop adds is a
  mask of six tests a block of 64 records, of bytes it has.
- **The cleanups' search** is a binary search a `return`, in the units
  that have a cleanup at all (none of the four corpora). The first form
  walked every record before the token, a unit's returns times its
  cleanups; each record keeps the furthest end up to it, made as the
  reader appends it.
- **The asm labels' rule** is a bit an object in the loop over the
  declarations that makes the objects' facts already, at the machine's
  begin: no pass more.
- **The weak names' search** is eight records a step, asked once a
  declaration's first use in a unit that notes a weak name, and again at
  every use of a declaration whose name is noted. The reader's own asks,
  an identifier each in such a unit, are the same search now, where they
  were a loop.
