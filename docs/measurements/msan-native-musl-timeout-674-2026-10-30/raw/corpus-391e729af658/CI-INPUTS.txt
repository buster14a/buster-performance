CI input snapshot for buster14a/buster

This archive contains the original headers, libc, SQLite, and generated musl
header overlay from the Benchpress preprocessor-391e729af658 corpus. Input
bytes and historical manifests have not changed. The archive omits historical
compiler sources, measurements, and discovery output. README.txt records exact
origins; headers/package-owners.txt and headers/provenance.tsv retain per-file
package provenance. Historical absolute paths are records, not required paths.

The inputs retain their component terms and notices. The project license does
not replace those terms. In particular, libc/musl/COPYRIGHT, libc/glibc/LICENSES,
libc/mingw/COPYING, libc/wasi/LICENSE*, source/header notices, and LICENSES/ are
retained. SQLite's amalgamation states its public-domain dedication.

LICENSES/LLVM.txt is the unmodified retained LLVM notice from buster's LICENSES.
LICENSES/Zig.txt is from ziglang/zig commit d03a147ea0a590ca711b3db07106effc559b0fc6.
The unmodified GNU GPL 2 and LGPL 2.1 texts are from GNU's old-licenses site.
Linux-syscall-note is the Linux kernel's syscall-header exception text.

Consumers verify the archive's SHA-256 and the original per-directory manifests
before using the files. The historical Clang 23.1.1 binary hash remains a
provenance record; hosted CI intentionally uses LLVM's current Clang 23 packages.
