## A summary node's side facts on the window

Part of #2850 (step 2), the piece after the one below, on the same branch. [The specification](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/2850#issuecomment-7053609091), [its census and the survey of what reads below a root](https://github.com/buster14a/buster-performance/tree/main/docs/measurements/summary-facts-census-2850-2026-11-20), [the record](https://github.com/buster14a/buster-performance/tree/main/docs/measurements/summary-facts-2850-2026-11-21).

> **It is exact for what it makes and its faults are caught. One gate is refuted on one corpus, and two are over without being refuted.** The sweep reads 0.27, 0.31 and 0.31 ticks a record swept on a chunk's second run; with it the rows' sweeps read +0.34 and +0.39 more on `sqlite3.c` and Lua where 0.30 was asked (F3). By the chunk the rows' sweeps' first run less their second rose by +23, +115 and +232 ticks (+46, +144 and +259 in a second set) where 100 was asked and 200 refutes: **refuted on musl**, whose chunks are 127 records (F4). A `switch`'s selector's type, which the specification named, is not made ([#3200](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3200)). **And the four inputs were not enough to call it exact:** after the code was first frozen, the earlier pieces' one-statement programs and a generator of random ones found two differences here, both fixed in this piece, and three holes in earlier pieces' code, filed as #3201, #3202 and #3203. The piece runs in the shadow alone, so no compile reads a tick more.

### What it is

Step 2 ends with one summary node in the graph where a taken expression's nodes were. Every pass after the reader that reads below a full expression's root then needs its answer from that node's value or a side record of it. A survey of today's code says what a compile with the default warnings reads there; this piece makes it on the window, for each expression whose tape X makes whole, and compares it after the pipeline with the program's own walks on that root (`measure-node-machine --shadow --window-rows`). **No compile takes any of it.**

- **Its references** (`check_program_references`: the static objects its leaves name). A sweep of masks, 64 records a step (`node_rows_window_summary`), lists the chunk's by a table of the flags a record of each kind needs, and writes the count of the references through each record, in a register: one multiplication within a quadword and three steps of sums across them. An expression's references are the list's between two of those counts, two loads where its summary's record is written. A gather writes each listed record's object, eight a step.
- **Its root's return fact** (`check_program_return_facts`: a stop, an unknown). A call of a plain function's name has none; a function's declaration's line has a bit for that and one for noreturn, the tests' sweep copies a name's leaf's line's byte, and the tests' loop gives each block a mask of the other calls. Those are asked a record at a time (one record in 1,261, 719 and 3,810 of those swept), and an expression with a call that stops under an `&&`, an `||` or a `?:` has the flow's own reduction.
- **A condition's truth and a constant's fold** are the root's value's to answer. The window lists the two cases where the program's walk knows a truth the value has not: an `&&` or an `||` whose second operand's truth is known (the rows' turns), and a cast that passes a truth its own value has not, `(char)(long)&x` (C's loop).
- **`--no-window-summaries`** is the cost's switch, and the control (`BUSTER_NODE_READER_WINDOW_SUMMARY_FAULT`) changes what reader time keeps, one fact an expression in turn.

### Two fixes it carries

- **A cast that passes a truth** (found by an earlier piece's probe run again, `if ((char)(long)&x)`): the checker's walk of a branch's condition gives a cast its operand's truth where the cast's value has none ([#3204](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3204): clang keeps both edges). A summary node has its root's value alone, so C's loop lists such a cast's expression where its consumer may ask its truth. On the four inputs it lists no expression.
- **A constant of an object's record** (found by the generator; the values' piece, #3197, merged): `(_Bool)&x` is an integer's constant, 1, whose record still has x for its object, and the value's column took a record's object wherever it had one. It takes it where the record's constant is an address's. `tests/checker/sources/0013.c` has that statement; no corpus has.

### What else the generator and the regression programs found

Not this piece's, and filed: the reader-time tests take an ordered comparison of a pointer with an integer, and a cast of an array or a function to a narrower integer, with their warning ([#3201](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3201)); a `return` under a pending cleanup is taken as a plain return ([#3202](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3202)); a multiversioned function's object has no declaration at reader time ([#3203](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3203)). On the code here the generator's 90,000 programs read 212 that differ, every one #3201's; of the 408 regression programs that run with no option, 9 end with status 1, #3202's and #3203's.

### What it means

- **The side facts a compile with the default warnings reads are exact on the window**: an expression's references and its root's return fact as a side record, and a condition's truth and a constant's fold by its root's value, with two listings. A `switch`'s condition stays the walk's.
- **Their work a record is a third of a tick, and over its gate:** the sweep reads 0.27, 0.31 and 0.31 ticks a record swept on a chunk's second run, 4%, 5% and 4% of what the rows' sweeps read without it (6.27, 6.71 and 8.14). What the consumer adds, two loads an expression where its summary's record is written, is the first compile's to read.
- **By the chunk it pays a cold start of its own, and on musl that is past what its gate allows:** the sweep's first run reads 0.41, 0.80 and 1.70 a record swept, and its first run less its second 68, 185 and 167 ticks a chunk, beside the 1342, 2163 and 2044 the rows' other sweeps pay there already ([#3196](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3196)). In the shadow the reference's sweeps run between a chunk's and the next's; a compile that takes expressions runs none of them there, and is where a chunk's first run is to be read.
- **Four inputs and a fixture are not what "exact" asks.** Five differences were in none of them: a truth through a cast and a constant of an object's record here, and in earlier pieces' code what the reader-time tests take with a warning, a `return` under a cleanup and a multiversioned function's object. A generator of programs and the repository's own regression programs found them in minutes. Both are in the record, and a piece's freeze runs them from here on.

**What is next:** the earlier pieces' three holes ([#3201](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3201), [#3202](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3202), [#3203](https://github.com/buster14a/buster/issues/3203)), which a compile that takes expressions cannot have, with a trigger of every warning the tests stand for run through the shadow; then that compile, behind an option, where the sweeps' cost by the chunk is read.
