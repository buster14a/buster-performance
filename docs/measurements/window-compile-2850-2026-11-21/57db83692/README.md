# Sparse pages for inherited declaration origins

The [source at 57db83692](implementation.patch) replaces the eager 32-bit origin column with one-byte origin classifications and stores the positions of rare inherited origins in sparse pages. Three independent native rounds per series compare true pristine b86eadcbd against its direct child on frozen [SQLite](sqlite/README.md) and the [Buster compiler source](main/README.md). Every timed command produced a normal relocatable object. Both ordinary and `-Wall -Wextra` output were checked before timing in each corpus.

The [independent origin census](source-qualification/source-density.tsv) counted 716,137 SQLite positions, including 6,857 self origins and two inherited ones. Removing initialization entirely caused reads of unwritten data on the compiler and musl workloads. The [observer patch](source-qualification/origin-observer-complete.patch), [summarizer](source-qualification/summarize-origin.py) and [earlier layout patch](source-qualification/origin-byte.patch) accompany the [exact measured source](implementation.patch). A separate [three-policy campaign](source-qualification/corpus.tsv.gz) compared all 4,116 relocatable objects and diagnostics on the production source. The earlier layout also retained all [3,939 tracked fixture outcomes](source-qualification/precursor-tracked.json).

The SQLite candidate was slower by 0.2875 ms at the paired median and in all three wall pairs, despite fewer minor faults. Compiling Buster itself took 6.1218 more total cycles/token at the median and regressed in all three PMU pairs.
