Membership and candidates:
 Preserve all original hot membership, required names, unique owned-name,
 type/extent, ELF type, approved shift and section/address checks. The index
 admits every ordinary SHT_SYMTAB entry before section filtering; DYNSYM is
 excluded. Full key remains name/type/bind/other/size. Complete equal-key
 bands are counted before validity/address/section filtering. Missing bands
 refuse. Every candidate in an owned key band must be defined in an ordinary
 PROGBITS ALLOC|EXEC section; both declared extent and actual section bytes
 must cover its extent, and its last-byte address must be representable.
 Undefined/reserved/ABS, NOBITS, unsupported, nonexec, short bytes, overflow
 and malformed outside candidates refuse rather than disappear.

First pass — mandatory anchors:
 A member is an anchor exactly when its full-key band contains one symbol
 in BOTH images. Validate every such anchor; do not skip inconsistent ones
 or choose a majority. It must have exact requested image delta, checked
 subtraction of the object offset and shift, and establish the same normal
 and variant contribution base as every other anchor. Use explicit
 has_anchor because base zero is valid. At least one anchor is required.
 A key unique in one image only cannot establish an anchor.

Second pass — qualified ownership:
 Preserve page alignment and last-byte contribution range checks. Require
 one full file-backed executable section range for normal (base, hot_size)
 and variant (base, hot_size + shift). Calculate expected member addresses
 with checked additions, rejecting unrepresentable zero-size endpoints.
 Count all full-key candidates at expected address before section filtering;
 require exactly one and then require the selected full-range section.
 Retain selected pointers per member for all later identity checks; never
 re-enter an unqualified globally unique lookup.

 Extra equal-key candidates may only be valid disjoint functions outside the
 complete contribution, including the variant prefix. For nonzero extent
 (a,n) and contribution (B,L), overlap is a>=B ? a-B<L : n>B-a. This avoids
 unchecked exclusive endpoints and preserves valid last-byte UINT64_MAX.
 Extra zero-size candidates are points: a>=B && a-B<=L means overlap,
 conservatively including a representable upper endpoint. The sole selected
 owned zero at an endpoint is allowed when its expected address is
 representable. Same-address duplicate candidates always refuse. Different
 full-key aliases keep existing behavior; no STT_FILE or first-match rule.

Preservation:
 Keep all prefix, full suffix/nonoperand bytes, relocation width/extent/
 overlap, member/required, allocation bounds and clear-on-failure gates.
 Keep hp_elf80c fixed. Public proof layout stays unchanged; only private
 candidate bands and retained-resolution storage are needed. The interface
 comment may describe qualified ownership. PT_LOAD, runtime, numeric
 relocation operand semantics and absolute performance remain unproved.

Validation is limited to unchanged linked_controls.c plus new adversarial
 linked geometry controls, strict and ASan/UBSan/leaks modes, built from an
 immutable all-dependency source snapshot with pre/post checks. Genuine
 shared cores is invoked outside env-i with inherited XDG. Actual compiler,
 flags, commands, statuses and source/binary hashes are retained. Independent
 actual-source/control review is required before any further disposition.
