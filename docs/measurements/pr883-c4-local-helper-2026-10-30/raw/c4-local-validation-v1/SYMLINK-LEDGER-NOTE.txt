SYMLINKS.nul contains native find path-NUL-target-NUL pairs, without sorting
the individual fields. The earlier draft incorrectly sorted NUL fields
separately; its exact bytes remain as SYMLINKS-UNPAIRED-DRAFT.nul and are not
the authoritative path/target mapping. Both actual symlinks target /dev/null.
No link or generated fixture input was changed during this metadata curation.
