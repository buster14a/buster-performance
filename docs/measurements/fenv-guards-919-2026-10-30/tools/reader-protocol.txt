Original #919 shipping time-runs reader

Build:
  clang -std=c17 -O2 -Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror -Wconversion -Wshadow -Wstrict-prototypes -Wmissing-prototypes read-results.c -o read-results

Run:
  ./read-results results > medians.csv
  ./read-results results --samples > samples.csv

Expected raw files:
  results/time-p{1,2,3}-s{0,64,256}.log
Each is one first-party time-runs invocation: 21 measured runs, 2 warm-ups,
2 newly created executable copies per build, 3 argument lists, base and head,
and exactly one executable placement per build in that invocation. Lists are
1 SQLite, 2 ACCESS off, 3 ACCESS on. Requested placement shifts are filename
labels; separate binary proof receipts establish their actual instructions.

Every log must contain exactly 252 distinct measured (run,list,build,copy)
records and one final "time-runs: done, 0 failed processes" line. All nine
logs must validate before output. There are 2,268 measured processes in total.
Each record must have exit status 0, positive wall time at the producer's
printed one-microsecond precision, and positive user cycles and instructions.
Wall/user/system time is bounded to one hour per process. Counters are bounded
to INT64_MAX. The reader requires an extended-precision long double with at
least 64 binary precision bits, ensuring exact integer and half-integer medians
within those bounds. Exact integer parsing checks overflow before arithmetic.

Unknown lines, malformed records, duplicate/missing tuples or files, failed
or uncounted warnings, malformed/duplicate/missing completion lines, and any
text after completion refuse with exit 1 and no CSV. Known first-party memory,
per-copy summary and comparison shapes are allowed; their numeric medians are
non-authoritative. The reader recomputes every result from measured raw rows.
No warm-up rows are expected on success: the producer only prints failures.

Default output contains 54 process/shift/source/build rows. Each pools 42 raw
samples (21 per copy) and reports arithmetic-even median wall/cycles/
instructions, both copies' individual 21-sample medians, and matched-cell head
percent changes. First-party time-runs uses the upper-middle value for its
even-sized pooled summary, so reader medians can differ from those summaries.
The --samples form contains all 2,268 individual raw rows. Wall ns retain the
printed 1us precision; they do not reconstruct producer sub-microsecond data.

The reader validates log structure and counts. Caller-owned source/compiler/
input manifests and placement proof receipts bind the measured cohort. In
particular, the primary #919 cohort uses the official c336 Clang 23.1.4 archive;
the host-default b538 cohort is supplemental only. This reader performs no
host measurement, target execution, or source branch changes.

Independent fixture writer and test-reader.sh preserve positive arithmetic
controls and refusals for missing/duplicate records, bad status, zero metrics,
missing/extra files, warnings, completion failure, integer overflow, malformed
record prefixes, and unknown/trailing text. Test with optimized and ASan/UBSan
reader binaries; all controls must pass before applying the reader to evidence.

Completed local verification:
  analysis/controls/timing-reader-fixtures.csv contains all 25 modes on both builds.
  Four positive modes produce the expected 54 median rows; 21 negative modes
  each return 1 with zero CSV bytes. The optional raw output contains exactly
  2,268 rows. Empty CLI and unknown-option checks also refuse without CSV.
  Optimized strict-warning and ASan/UBSan builds pass all checks. A separate
  read-only reviewer and an auxiliary-grammar reviewer found no remaining
  issue in the final bounded parser/median calculation. These are parser
  checks, not compiler correctness or performance measurements.

Process 2 reverses the producer's build order: head is its comparison reference
and base is reported against head. Processes 1 and 3 use base as reference.
The auxiliary grammar enforces this policy; all CSV head/base changes are
independently recomputed from the raw samples. Reversed-producer positive and
wrong-reference negative fixtures exercise this distinction.

The genuine first-party reversed-order control is retained separately under
analysis/controls/time-p2-s0.log (SHA a7baac2a230a6f0b97e8624b2dca5c7094517a68a05771b8110cd66165007919).
Its 252 real successful samples were inserted into an otherwise synthetic
nine-file FORMAT control. All six real pooled medians were independently
recomputed and matched. That hybrid CSV is only parser evidence; none of its
values is a primary compiler-performance result.

Primary producer logs are written directly to their canonical filenames.
Input checks, timestamps, exit status and raw hashes are retained in separate
bindings sidecars; lock-wait diagnostics are separate again. The full corpus
and runtime/tool/header ledgers are checked before and after the campaign.
