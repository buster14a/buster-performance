#919 evidence-only call guard controls

All tools/fixtures here are original Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception material.
The source adapter is diagnostic instrumentation, never a shipping change or a
timing input. It leaves the original floating-environment helper body intact,
inserts atomic helper-entry ACCESS-on/off and preexisting-diagnostic counters,
and records each of its two source call routes. The one exit receipt comes only
from BUSTER_UNIT_CHECKER. Its total must equal both ACCESS categories and the
sum of the compute/builtin route counts.

Calls counted are source-executed entries of the adapted production checker
unit. Shipping disassembly must independently verify the corresponding actual
helper call sites and the guard branches. Exact object/stdout/diagnostic parity
binds the diagnostic run's produced output to the shipping compiler. These
counts are not hardware measurements of the unmodified binary. No instrumented
latency or instructions may be substituted for the shipping measurements.

The helper build and control tests are short local jobs. Checker rebuilds and
host executions require the parent's coordinated cores/shared-lock scheduling.
build-probe.sh preserves the retained production checker command, requires its
compiler identity to match the explicit official compiler, changes only its
output object path, and compiles copied sources. It relinks that object with the
other40 original objects. Every shipping source, all41 retained objects, tool and
adapted source identity is hashed before compilation and checked unchanged after.
It makes no stack-protector-policy change. A prior host-default-Clang retained
command intentionally fails its official-compiler gate.

The reader rejects absent/duplicate/malformed/signed/overflowing receipts, bad
category/route sums, lost ACCESS-on calls, remaining head ACCESS-off calls and
unknown modes. off mode requires base>0, base.on0 and head.total0; on requires
retained on calls and both sources exercising the builtin route; mixed requires
both base categories; invalid-on additionally preserves nonzero entry-diagnostic
counts. All modes require head.on==base.on and head.off0.

The initial short controls include two successful original-source adaptations,
six adapter rejection cases, four valid count modes, and thirteen reader
rejection cases. All25 passed; tool-control-results/before-tests.sha256 and its
after-tests-check.txt bind those executions. No compiler checker was built by
that tool-control run.

tiny-off.c and tiny-on.c have identical bytes except OFF/ON on the pragma line.
They exercise floating arithmetic/conversion plus builtin unary and overflow
routes; their runtime results are exact under the ordinary default environment.
tiny-scope.c adds nested OFF and restored ON scopes. invalid-on/off.c require
matching semantic-error status/diagnostics and no object. strict-rounding.c
actually sets upward rounding, evaluates an ON halfway sum and checks inexact,
then restores nearest rounding before executing its OFF scope. Its external
Clang helper is explicitly ACCESS-on and uses system fenv constants. All valid
objects must link and exit0 for scalar/vector and both sources. The control
runner requires shipping/probe stdout, diagnostics (apart from the sole receipt)
and objects to match, then base/head objects to match for each same input.
