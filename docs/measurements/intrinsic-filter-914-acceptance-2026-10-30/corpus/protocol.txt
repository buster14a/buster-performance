Timing completed

Measure every one of the same 220 units, numeric 00001 through 00220. None is selected from observed performance. Source sizes range from 26 to 17,179 bytes. This includes minimal expressions and aggregate, floating, long-double, header, libc/math and varargs cases.

Existing first-party C time-runs in the candidate harness invokes each baseline/candidate as a separate process: one -c invocation per unit, one warm-up and three measured rounds, and one freshly made executable copy per build. All slots rotate by round. It reports each child's wall/user/system time, faults, peak RSS, actual user-mode PMU cycles and instructions. The same source/output path and environment apply to both. No TSC conversion is used.

Each timed invocation must exit zero and provide nonzero scheduled counters. Corpus correctness is established separately above; time-runs' matching-exit-status rule alone is insufficient. The original C c-testsuite-aggregate.c requires exactly one successful base/head/copy-1 record for every unit in every round. It rejects missing, duplicate, malformed, failed, uncounted, signed or overflowing fields. It sums each round's 220 whole-process wall times and user PMU cycles/instructions. Summed wall values retain the raw report's 1 microsecond precision; they are not the elapsed duration of the whole batch or reconstructed higher-resolution timestamps.

Existing time-runs rejects unavailable or multiplexed counters for its comparisons. The aggregate additionally rejects zero counts. Optimized and ASan/UBSan controls accept known totals and reject seven incomplete/error classes.
