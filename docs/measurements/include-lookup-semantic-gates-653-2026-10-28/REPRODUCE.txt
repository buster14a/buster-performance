The temporary namespace belongs to the probe. Only its forked child changes
RLIMIT_NOFILE; it opens descriptors until the table is full, compares open and
fstatat errors for one absent path, then exits. The parent remains unaffected.
The other fixture replaces an ancestor after pinning its leaf directory and
compares new full-path versus old relative-descriptor reads and self-stat data.

Expected decisive lines on the recorded local Linux host:
directory-fd counterexample: ordinary=B pinned=A pinned_identity_and_times_unchanged=1
fd-limit counterexample: opened=60 open_result=-1 open_errno=24 stat_result=-1 stat_errno=2
design probes: setup_and_namespace_counterexample=1 resource_counterexample=1 ordinary_cost_checks=1

The opened count can differ with inherited descriptors; the errno/namespace
inequivalence is the gate. Exit zero means that both counterexamples were
reproduced, not that either optimization is correct. Cost medians are a small
synthetic eligibility screen on Ryzen7940HS/Linux7.3/Clang23.1.4, not an A/A
qualified compiler speedup or a 9700X result. No performance claim follows.
