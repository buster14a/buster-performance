# Raw retention and publication scope

The native raw archive is a complete transfer of its selected payload,
verified by the target digest and `raw-payload.sha256` after extraction.
It contains every native preflight/helper/whole/phase/gate stdout, stderr and
status, including the failed original header-layout gate; every strict
summary; generated diagnostic source/helpers; historical generated inputs;
the 841-file main dependency inventory and sizes; source/predefine identities;
and the complete eight native preprocessed outputs. The shared baseline
executable is retained by root's immutable campaign artifact, not copied into
this archive. The private proper-layout gate copy remains on the target and
has its identical SHA-256 in the receipts. The profiler's temporary per-cohort
copies are deleted by the original profiler after use, as documented in its
source; both roles shared the same temporary copy within their cohort.

The pinned SQL pack's full 786-path manifest (785 files plus predefines) is a
**postrun content inventory**, not a separately observed before/after pack
comparison. No symlinks were present. Main's dependency/source manifests were
generated before measurement and verified afterwards. These two scopes must
not be conflated in a curated report.

Local artifacts retain the earlier incomplete-header-pack failures, the first
direct-file helper version, the final full-inclusion sanitized helper, complete
local oracle outputs, reader protocol controls, tiny method-only timing log,
and the local duplicated phase-shape check. The latter two are not native
performance samples. Both final-audit attempts are retained: the first stops
on an old `exit=0` status spelling; the successful second accepts only the
explicit old/new status spellings and checks all expected statuses.

For a source/evidence Git publication, large ELF files, raw `-E` outputs,
8 MB generated inputs and the 27 MB compressed transfer may be represented
by their retained exact identities, generator/reproduction commands and a
private-location disposition rather than committed as giant Git blobs. Their
raw originals remain here or in the immutable shared campaign artifact. Do
not drop a failure/negative merely because a large binary/output is excluded.
All 504 raw timed rows, complete phase logs, correctness/status receipts,
dependency manifests, original C helper sources and summaries are small enough
to retain directly. Preserve component notices in generated source copies.

The report is a frozen e7 configuration accounting/completion record. It does
not qualify a future compiler PR head, change host settings, establish a new
source speedup, or replace the historical #462/#486 source-effect evidence.
