The branches' rows on the window (#2850)

X's rows on the window listed every expression with an operator
evaluated as branches (a statement's condition over `&&` or `||`, and
the `!`, `&&` and `||` below it). X writes such an operator no entry,
and each value it tests its test (a narrow class's CONVERT to I32, a
CONST 0, NE) and a branch by its sense after the value's row. This
makes the kinds and the classes of those on the front's window
(measure-node-machine --shadow --window-rows). The branch's label is
an entry's value, which is not made yet. No compile takes any of it:
the shadow compares every tape made with X's on T's columns, entry by
entry.

- c_check_node_machine_rows.c: a tested value's test and its branch
  are its conversion's part's row, which is free (a logical operator
  converts its operand to its own type without its qualifiers): four
  new rows of the rows' table, a narrow class's or not, by the sense.
  An operator evaluated as branches has no shape and lists nothing.
- node_rows_window_updates, in a chunk with an operator that takes a
  turn: the update's sweep of masks compresses the chunk's `!`, `&&`
  and `||` evaluated as branches into a second list in the same pass,
  and a loop goes down it from its end (node_rows_window_branch),
  consumers before their operands. A turn reads its operator's sense
  from a byte column and gives each operand its own
  (node_rows_window_tested): a byte for an operand that is such an
  operator, and for a value the test's shape and its entries' number,
  with no branch on the operator. A statement's CONDITION takes no
  turn: the reader writes its sense at its operand's record
  (node_facts_reader_expression). The sweep's block loop is not
  unrolled: its first run over a chunk read less in one copy.
- Listed still: a tested value with a conversion of its own, a
  qualified _Bool's read, which the lowering converts to bool again
  (#3185).
- The listed expression's reason is by its record's own shape (an
  update by its template, its destination with no room by the mark),
  with no read of its consumer.
- The chunk's guard for the two lists counts the operators with a
  turn (C's loop's records evaluated as branches less its
  CONDITIONs).
- The shadow keeps a chunk's first run's streams and compares its
  second run's with them, byte for byte.
- The report counts the operators with a turn, and times the lists
  and the two loops as one part; test-checker part 5 has the
  fixture's condition lines (`&&`, `||` and `!` under an if, a while,
  a do and a for, nested in either operand; a tested value of each
  class and kind, a narrow class's bounds, the const bool's reads it
  lists, a bool's update that is tested, and four conditions 64 times
